## 6. Recommendation Logic (for the agent)

- Building/RCC contractor → bar bending + bar cutting combo, stirrup bender, needle vibrator, mini crane/monkey hoist, cube moulds.
- Road contractor → walk-behind or ride-on roller, plate compactor, rammer, concrete/groove cutter, thermoplastic paint + road safety items, geogrid/geocell for soil work.
- Surveyor / layout work → total station (budget: IM-55; premium: iX robotic) or auto level (B40A entry, B20 premium) + tripod + prism + staff.
- New QC lab / RMC plant → CTM + cube moulds + slump cone + sieves + oven bundle; mention NABL support.
- Rebaring/retrofitting → Fischer FIS V 360 / EM Plus 585 + dispenser gun.
- Waterproofing/flooring → admixtures, curing compound, sealants, power trowel.
- Always ask: project type, site city, quantity, timeline, budget range — then capture name + phone as a lead and offer sales-team callback for exact pricing.
